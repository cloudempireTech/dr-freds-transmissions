Who We Are

The Transmission Specialists You Can Trust

Founded with a clear mission: to provide the drivers and fleet operators of Spring, The Woodlands, Tomball, and North Houston with honest, reliable, and scientifically verified transmission services.

Modern transmissions have evolved from purely hydraulic assemblies into complex, computer-managed mechatronic units with multi-stage planetary gears, variable valve bodies, and microsecond electronic shift solenoids. We invest continuously in dealer-level computerized scanners, dynamometer testing stations, and certified training to service everything from classic 3-speed automatics to 10-speed transmissions and commercial dual-clutch drivetrains.

The ATRA Code of Ethics

As an Automatic Transmission Rebuilders Association member, we adhere to the Golden Rule: We treat every customer with the transparency, fairness, and craftsmanship we would demand for our own families.

Our Rebuild Standards

Why Our Rebuilds Outlast Factory OEM

We don’t just swap worn parts — we address factory design flaws and upgrade vital components.

Upgraded Heavy-Duty Friction Clutches

Every rebuild replaces stock clutch materials with reinforced Raybestos or Alto red friction plates capable of handling elevated heat and towing loads.

Sonnax Valve Body Modifications

We re-ream valve bores and install oversized hard-anodized valves to eliminate hydraulic cross-leaks and prevent common line pressure drops.

Furnace-Brazed Torque Converters

Factory crimped turbine blades flex under load. Our converters feature furnace-brazed fins and hardened steel splines for rock-solid lockup.
