Accurate Pinpointing

Know The Exact Cause Before Authorizing Work

Over 40% of transmission complaints in late-model vehicles do not require a full rebuild. Instead, they stem from faulty throttle position sensors, corroded wiring harnesses, sticky shift solenoids, or contaminated fluid.

At Dr. Fred’s Transmissions, our diagnostic protocol leaves no room for guesswork:

  • Multi-Module Telemetry Scan: Scanning TCM, ECM, ABS, and BCM modules for interlinked communications codes.
  • Line Pressure Port Gauging: Direct manual mechanical pressure testing across idle, stall, and reverse ranges.
  • Fluid Pan Inspection: Removing the pan to check for fine friction dusting versus sharp metallic shavings.
  • Road Dynamometer Simulation: Real-time road test recording shift points, slippage percentages, and lockup clutch timing.
Preventive Protection

Complete Fluid Exchange & Cooler Cleanse

Heat is the #1 killer of automatic transmissions. Operating at just 20°F over normal operating temperature can cut transmission fluid life in half.

Our full-system fluid service completely flushes stale, thermally oxidized fluid from the torque converter, valve channels, and radiator cooler lines, replenishing your system with synthetic OEM-specified ATF.
