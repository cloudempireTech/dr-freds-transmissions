Heart of the Automatic

In-House Torque Converter Rebuilding

The torque converter couples your engine's power to the transmission via hydraulic fluid flow. A shuddering lockup clutch or broken stator can destroy an entire transmission within dozens of miles.

  • Multi-Disk Lockup Clutches: Premium carbon-woven friction linings engineered to withstand intense towing and trailer hauling.
  • Furnace-Brazed Impeller & Turbine: High-heat furnace welding prevents internal fin detachment and harmonic cavitation.
  • Dynamic High-RPM Balancing: Computerized spun balancing eliminates drivetrain shudder and vibration at all highway speeds.
4WD & AWD Systems

Transfer Cases & Differential Service

Texas trucks and commercial haulers demand bulletproof 4-wheel drive systems. We repair stretched transfer case chains, worn shift forks, cracked cases from pump-rub, and howling differential bearings.

Whether you drive an electronic shift-on-the-fly Ford Super Duty, Chevy Silverado Z71, Jeep Rubicon, or full-time AWD SUV, our master technicians restore smooth, quiet power transfer to all four wheels.
